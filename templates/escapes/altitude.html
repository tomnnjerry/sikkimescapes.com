Short answer

Below 2,500 m most people feel nothing. Between 2,500 and 3,500 m (Lachung, Lachen) sleep well, go slowly and drink water. Above 3,500 m (Yumthang, Tsomgo, Nathu La, Gurudongmar) visit by day and sleep lower. Headache, nausea or breathlessness at rest mean go down.

Climb in steps

Spend your first night around Gangtok (about 1,650 m) before Lachung or Lachen. Above 3,000 m, try not to raise the height you sleep at by more than about 500 m a night, and add a rest day for every 1,000 m.

Visit high, sleep low

Gurudongmar (about 5,150 m), Zero Point and Nathu La are day visits. Go early, stay an hour or less at the top, and come back down to sleep. Never try Gurudongmar the same day you arrive from Gangtok.

Know the warning signs

Mild headache, poor sleep and breathlessness on stairs are common. Confusion, a bad headache that does not ease, vomiting, breathlessness at rest or unsteady walking mean descend at once and see a doctor.

Small things that help

Drink water steadily, eat well, skip alcohol on the first high nights and walk slowly. Keep warm: cold makes everything harder. Ask your doctor before the trip about medicines; do not take them on a stranger's say-so.

Children and older travellers

Children under five are not allowed into North Sikkim's protected areas. For young children and older travellers we plan lower overnight stops and shorter high days. Check current status before you travel.

Insurance

Hold travel insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation at the heights on your route. Some policies exclude travel above a set height, so read the small print.
