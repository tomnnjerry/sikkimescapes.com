Short answer

You tell us dates, people and budget. Within one working day we send a day-by-day plan and an itemised price. You change what you like, pay a deposit to book, and we confirm every room, car and permit in writing.

Six steps

What happens when

  1. You tell us the basics

    Dates (or a month), how many people and their ages, roughly what you want to spend, and anything you already know you want: Gurudongmar, a homestay, a monastery festival. A form, an email or a WhatsApp message all work.

  2. We send a plan and a price

    Within one working day: a day-by-day route with drive times and altitudes, the named hotels or homestays, the car, every permit and fee, and our planning fee as its own line.

  3. You change it until it fits

    Move up or down a tier, add a night, swap a district. Each change comes back with a new itemised price, so you can see what it costs.

  4. You book with a deposit

    Accept the quote in writing and pay the deposit. We confirm every service in writing. The payments policy sets out when the balance is due.

  5. We arrange the permits

    Protected areas such as Tsomgo, Nathu La, the Silk Route and North Sikkim need permits issued through Sikkim Tourism-registered agents. We apply through registered partners, usually a day ahead, with the ID and photos we ask you for. Foreign nationals apply for their own Restricted Area Permit online on e-FRRO; we tell you how. Check current status before you travel.

  6. You travel with one person to call

    Your driver meets you at Bagdogra or NJP. If a road closes or a pass shuts, we reroute you and tell you what it changes, including the price.

What a price includes

  • Rooms, with the meals stated
  • Car and driver, or shared-jeep seats
  • Permits and fees, including the ₹50 Sikkim entry fee
  • Our planning fee, shown separately

What it leaves out

  • Flights or trains to Bagdogra or NJP
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips, heaters and personal spending

Prices are indicative until quoted

Website prices are "from" prices per person, twin sharing. Your written quote is the offer, valid for the days it states.

Facts that change are flagged

Permits, fees, road openings and festival dates move every year. We say so on every page and check again before you travel.

Stories come with sources

We name the book, article or institution behind each story, or say "as told locally". We never invent legends.

Photos are credited

Every photograph comes from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence, with its author named on our photo credits page.

No made-up praise

We do not publish reviews, ratings or client numbers we cannot show you. Ask us for references instead.

Your data stays with the trip

We share only what a hotel, driver or permit office needs. See the privacy policy.
