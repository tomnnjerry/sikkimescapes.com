Short answer

A Gangtok weekend with shared jeeps and a budget hotel can cost under ₹10,000 per person from NJP. A 5–6 night trip that includes North Sikkim usually starts around ₹13,000–20,000 per person on a budget. Every price here is indicative, per person, twin sharing, and every package shows how it breaks down. Check current status before you travel.

Spend less

Six ways to bring the price down

None of these make the trip worse. Most make it slower and more local, which is usually better.

  1. Share the car: four to six people in one vehicle cuts the biggest cost per head.
  2. Use shared jeeps for long transfers such as NJP to Gangtok, priced per seat.
  3. Sleep in homestays at about ₹1,200–2,500 per person with meals.
  4. Travel from June to mid-September or in late January and February, outside the New Year week.
  5. Stay two nights per stop. Fewer moves means fewer car days.
  6. Budget the extras: the ₹50 Sikkim tourist fee, Zero Point cab charges, heaters and tips.
