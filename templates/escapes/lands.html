Short answer

For high lakes, snow and yak country go north. For Gangtok, Tsomgo Lake and Nathu La, the east. For Kangchenjunga views, monasteries and the Goecha La trail, the west. For tea, warm valleys and easy family days, the south. Pakyong holds the Old Silk Route above Zuluk, and Soreng the Barsey rhododendron forest.

Sikkim reorganised from four districts to six in December 2021: Pakyong was carved from East Sikkim and Soreng from West Sikkim, and the old four were renamed Gangtok, Mangan, Gyalshing and Namchi. Most travellers still say North, East, West and South Sikkim, so we use both names.
