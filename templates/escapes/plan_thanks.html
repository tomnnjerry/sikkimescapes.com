What happens now

Three things, in this order

  1. We check your dates. Permits, road status and room availability for the districts you picked.
  2. You get a plan and a price. Every night, every drive, and the cost of rooms, car, permits and our fee on separate lines.
  3. We adjust it with you. Change hotels, nights or tier until it fits. No payment until you decide to book.

While you wait

Send ID and two passport photos per traveller once your dates are fixed if your trip includes North Sikkim, Tsomgo, Nathu La or the Silk Route; protected-area permits need them. Children under 5 are not permitted in North Sikkim protected areas. Check current status before you travel.
