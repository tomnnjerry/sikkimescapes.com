How we rate

Best, good and not now

Every place carries a 12-month rating from the Gangtok desk. Best means clear weather, open roads and the reason you came (flowers, snow, views or a festival). Good means worth it with a caveat, such as cloud or cold. Places not shown are closed by snow, cut off by monsoon slides, or simply better another time. Roads and permits change every year: check current status before you travel.
