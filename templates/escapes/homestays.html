Short answer

A Sikkim homestay usually costs about ₹1,200–2,500 per person a night with dinner and breakfast. Rooms are simple and clean, meals are what the family cooks, and the money stays in the village. It is the cheapest way to stay in Sikkim and the best way to meet the people who live there. Rates are indicative: check current status before you travel.

What you get

  • A clean private room, sometimes with its own bathroom
  • Dinner and breakfast cooked by the family
  • Local knowledge: walks, monasteries, the farm
  • A quiet night in a village, not a bazaar

What to expect

  • Hot water by bucket in some houses in winter
  • Early dinners and few menu choices
  • Weak mobile signal in some valleys
  • Cash only in many villages: carry enough

Good manners

How to be a good guest

  1. Take your shoes off at the door and ask before you photograph people, the family altar or the inside of a prayer room.
  2. Eat what is cooked, or tell us about allergies and diets beforehand so the family can plan.
  3. Pay the agreed rate. If you want to give more, buy honey, cardamom or weaving from the family.
  4. Keep the evenings quiet; most villages sleep early and farm at dawn.
  5. Carry your plastic back down, and carry a refillable bottle.
