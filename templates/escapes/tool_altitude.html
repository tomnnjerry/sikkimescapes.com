Acclimatising, plainly

  • Above about 3,000 m, try not to raise your sleeping altitude by more than 500 m a night. Day trips higher are fine if you come back down to sleep.
  • Take it slowly the first afternoon up high. Walk, do not run up steps.
  • Drink water steadily and skip alcohol and sleeping pills for the first two nights up high.
  • At the high lakes and passes (Gurudongmar, Nathu La, Zero Point) keep your stop short and warm.
  • Headache, nausea, dizziness or very poor sleep can mean altitude sickness. Do not go higher with symptoms; if they get worse, go down. Tell your driver: they turn back for this every season.
  • This is general advice, not medical advice. If you have heart or lung conditions, are pregnant, or are travelling with young children or older relatives, see a doctor before booking a high trip.
  • Children under 5 are not permitted in North Sikkim protected areas. Check current status before you travel.
