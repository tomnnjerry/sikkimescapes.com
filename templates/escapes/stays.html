Short answer

Budget rooms in Sikkim cost about ₹1,500–3,000 a night and village homestays about ₹1,200–2,500 per person with meals. Mid-range hotels with heating and views run higher, and the top hotels cost ₹12,000 a room and up. Price bands below are indicative: check current rates before you travel.

Village homestays

Sleep in the house, eat in the kitchen

Registered homestays in Lepcha, Bhutia, Limbu and Nepali-speaking villages are the cheapest way to stay in Sikkim and the way you learn the most. The money goes to the family.

Homestays we bookAffordable Sikkim
  • Rooms are simple and clean; some houses share a bathroom
  • Dinner and breakfast are usually included, cooked by the family
  • Hot water can be a bucket in winter, and nights are cold above 2,000 m
  • We book directly and pay fairly, with no commission on top
