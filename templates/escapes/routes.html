Before you plan a day on the road

Sikkim's roads are slow and change with the weather. Shared jeeps are the cheapest way between towns, a reserved car is the most flexible, and protected areas need a registered vehicle and a permit. NH10 from Siliguri closes after monsoon slides; the fallback is via Kalimpong or Algarah. Check current status before you travel.
