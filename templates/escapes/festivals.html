How dates work

Most Sikkim festivals follow the Tibetan or Lepcha lunar calendar, so they move by two to four weeks from year to year. We list the usual months; ask us for the exact date in the year you travel. Monasteries are places of worship: dress modestly, ask before photographing and keep out of the dance ground.
