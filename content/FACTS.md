# Facts memo: use these, do not contradict them

Compiled 30 Sep 2026 – 4 Oct 2026 from the earlier Sikkim research (sikkimtourpackages/research/facts-memo.md)
for Sikkim. Anything here that is a rule, fee, road status or date is changeable: when you use it, add
"check current status before you travel". If your own research disagrees with this file, use the more cautious
wording and list the conflict in your hand-off report.

## Sikkim

- North Sikkim: Lachung, Yumthang, Zero Point reopened 8 Sep 2025. Lachen and Gurudongmar reopened 9 Mar 2026
  (new Bailey bridge over Taram Chu), closed again April 2026 after a landslide, road repaired 29 Aug 2026,
  permits resumed around 11–12 Sep 2026. Tourist vehicles must cross the Toong check post by 4:00 pm.
  Permits only through Sikkim Tourism-registered agents and vehicles. Operators require a minimum 2-night
  North Sikkim stay. Children under 5 are not permitted in North Sikkim protected areas.
- Chola and Doklam opened to domestic tourists from 27 Sep 2025 (limited vehicles per day).
- Nathu La (4,310 m): Indian nationals only; permit about ₹200 per person through a registered agent, applied a
  day ahead with ID and photos; usually open Wednesday to Sunday, closed Monday and Tuesday; snow closures.
- Tsomgo Lake: protected-area permit for Indians and foreigners through registered agents, usually bundled with
  Baba Mandir; foreigners get a one-day pass, groups of 2 or more.
- Zuluk / Old Silk Route: Indians only (permit via Rongli). Foreigners not allowed.
- Foreigners need a Restricted Area Permit to enter Sikkim (30 days, extendable by 30). Since 12 Jan 2026 there
  are no paper permits: apply online on the e-FRRO portal (indianfrro.gov.in) after arriving in India, 24–48 h
  approval. Foreigners may visit with a PAP (group of 2+, registered agent): Tsomgo, Lachung–Yumthang–Zero Point,
  Dzongri / Goecha La. Not allowed: Nathu La, Gurudongmar, Zuluk. Lachen / Thangu for foreigners unclear: say
  "ask us".
- Sikkim charges a ₹50 per tourist entry fee (Tourism Sustainable Development fee) since 14 Mar 2025,
  collected by hotels at check-in, valid 30 days, under-5s exempt.
- Pakyong Airport: no scheduled flights since June 2024. Plan on Bagdogra (IXB) or New Jalpaiguri (NJP).
- Sevoke–Rangpo railway: not open; completion targeted December 2027.
- NH10 (Siliguri–Rangpo–Gangtok) closes in the monsoon after slides; the alternative is via Kalimpong / Lava
  or Algarah. Say so on every route that uses NH10.
- Plastic water bottles: Lachung and Lachen local bodies have restricted packaged water bottles at times;
  carry a refillable bottle (say "carry a refillable bottle").

## People and words (use with care)

- Lepcha (Rong): considered the earliest inhabitants of Sikkim; Dzongu is a reserved Lepcha area in North
  Sikkim (permits needed for outsiders).
- Bhutia, Limbu (Tsong), Rai, Tamang, Gurung, Sherpa, Newar, Mangar and other Nepali-speaking communities.
- Kangchenjunga is worshipped in Sikkim as a guardian deity (Dzö-nga); Pang Lhabsol honours it.
- Do not invent folk tales. When you tell a story, name the source tradition and a checkable source (book,
  Wikipedia article, government page, museum). If a story is local tradition without a written source,
  say "as told locally" and keep it short.
